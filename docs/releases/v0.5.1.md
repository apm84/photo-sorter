## Photo Sorter 0.5.1: sync to the Family Photos app

- Family sync now goes to the Family Photos app, which moved out of the family calendar into its own app on the
  same Pi (port 8443). Change the address on the **Family sync** page to `https://<your Pi's name>:8443`; your
  pairing code keeps working. New pairing codes come from the Family Photos admin (`https://<your Pi's name>:8443/admin/`).
- If the old calendar address is still set, **Test connection** says the photos moved and shows the new address.
- Developers: the end-to-end sync test now runs against a real Family Photos receiver (`FAMILY_PHOTOS_SRC`, or
  `../family-photos` next to this repo).
