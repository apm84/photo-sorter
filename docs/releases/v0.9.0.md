## Photo Sorter 0.9.0: tidy pictures, album sorting, and faster sync while you use the PC

- **Pictures stay in their boxes.** The photo viewer now always fits the whole picture on screen (large previews
  used to be cut off at the bottom), and grids, album covers and suggestion covers can no longer be stretched by a
  big picture. A cover that can't be loaded shows an empty tile instead of a broken image.
- **Sort albums.** The Albums page and Suggested albums each have a **Sort** list: Name (or Newest), **Number of
  photos (high to low)**, and **Relevance / quality**. Relevance is a 0-100 score for how likely an album is a
  real, good event album: how many photos it has, how close together in time and place they are, how many show
  people you named, how large and sharp the photos are, how few screenshots, memes or duplicates it has and, for
  suggestions, how many photos show the scene in its name. Hover an album to see why it got its score.
- **Add to album from a suggestion.** When reviewing a suggested album, select photos and choose **Actions > Add to
  album...** to put them in an existing album or a new one. Albums use hard links, so no extra copies are made.
- **Family sync no longer stalls while you use the PC.** Each thumbnail and preview used to wait up to 20 seconds
  while you were using the keyboard or mouse, even when it was ready to send. Now only making a new preview waits.
