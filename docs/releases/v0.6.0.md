## Photo Sorter 0.6.0: videos, and Delete on selections

### Delete selected photos and videos
- Wherever you can select (Search, Videos, an album, a person), **Delete...** moves the selected *sorted copies* to the
  Recycle Bin (or `_index\_trash` when the Recycle Bin isn't available). It asks first, and **Undo delete** puts them
  back, album and People links included. Your original files are never touched, and later sort runs don't copy the
  deleted ones back. *Verify* lists their originals as "deleted on purpose".

### Videos
- Videos are no longer skipped. They're copied into the sorted folders with the same date and place rules as photos
  (the date from the video's own creation time, else the file time; the place from the GPS the phone wrote into it).
  Originals are only read: never changed, moved, deleted or re-encoded.
- New **Videos** tab: a grid with durations, a silent flip-book preview when you hover, its own search and filters
  (date, place, person, album, and Live Photo clips hidden by default).
- A player page with a seek bar that shows thumbnails as you scrub, the people in the video (click to jump to them),
  **Up next** with autoplay (a 5-second countdown you can cancel), and keyboard keys (Space/K, J/L, arrows, M, F).
- Videos that browsers can't play as they are (for example iPhone HEVC or old AVI clips) get a small H.264 copy in
  `_index\video`, made at low priority in the background; the original stays as it is. **Open original** always
  opens the untouched file.
- Faces: frames are sampled from each video and go through the same face detector, face recognition and People list
  as your photos, so a person you named shows up in their videos too.
- Content search: the same image AI looks at frames from each video, so searching "birthday cake" or "snow" finds
  videos as well as photos (videos are marked with their length).
- Duplicate videos (the same clip copied, re-encoded or made smaller) are found and shown in Clean up like photos.
- Videos can go into albums and be selected and deleted like photos.
- Family sync: full videos are sent to Family Photos **last**, after every photo, with the same speed limit and time
  window, in resumable chunks, each checked by SHA-256. Turn it off on the Family sync page (**Include videos**).
  Needs Family Photos 1.1 on the Pi (older versions simply get no videos).
- All video work follows the run mode (Background / Lowest priority / Full speed): ffmpeg runs at below-normal or idle
  priority and pauses with the rest of the work when you're using the PC.
- The installer now includes FFmpeg (LGPL build, about 100 MB more). See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

### Good to know
- The first run after updating reads every video once (details, a few frames, preview strip). Rough speed on a
  typical PC: 1 to 3 seconds per video to read, plus a few seconds per minute of video for the preview strip.
  H.264 copies take longer (roughly 3 to 15 times faster than real time, depending on the graphics card) and are
  only made for videos browsers can't play.
- *Settings*: **Include videos** in the app window. Without FFmpeg (source installs), videos are skipped as before.
