## Photo Sorter 0.7.0: Family sync sends only the albums you pick

- Family sync now sends **only the albums you pick** to CalendarPeace. Nothing else from your library is sent,
  and no single files.
- On the **Albums** page, select albums (click the circle on an album; Shift-click selects a range), then open
  **Actions** and choose:
  - **Sync with CalendarPeace**: the albums get a green **Synced** badge and go to the Pi on the next sync.
  - **Stop syncing**: their photos are removed from the Pi on the next sync, unless another synced album also
    has them.
  - **Delete...**: deletes the selected albums and their folders of links. Your photos stay in your library, and
    originals are never touched.
- Only the named people who appear in the synced photos are sent.
- With no album picked, the Pi ends up with no photos from Photo Sorter, and the status line says
  "Family sync: no albums selected for CalendarPeace".
- Press **Sync now** on the Family sync page to send your picks straight away (otherwise the next scheduled sync
  sends them).
