## Photo Sorter 0.8.0: suggested albums

- The **Albums** page now has **Suggested albums**. Press **Find suggestions** (it also looks after every sort run)
  and Photo Sorter groups your photos into events: outings, trips and gatherings, found from when and where the
  photos were taken.
- Each suggestion gets a name from what the photos show, the named people in them, the place and the date, for
  example "Beach day in Destin with Alex and Sam, Jul 12, 2024".
- Screenshots, memes, documents, graphics and videos are always left out. Events that are mostly in an album
  already aren't suggested.
- Open a suggestion to review it: select photos that don't belong and choose **Actions > Remove from suggestion**,
  **Rename** it, then **Keep as album** (a normal album, hard links, no extra space) or **Dismiss** it. Dismissed
  suggestions don't come back.
- It only reads what the sort run already worked out (no AI model runs again), so it takes seconds, even on an
  older PC. Everything stays on your PC, and originals are never touched.
