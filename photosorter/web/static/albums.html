Albums

To fill an album, select photos on the Search or People page (hover a photo and click its circle; Shift-click selects a range) and press Add to album. Each album is also a folder in your sorted library (Albums\<name>) holding links to the same photos, so it takes almost no extra space.

New album

Delete this album?

Only the album and its folder of links are deleted. Your photos stay in your library, and originals are never touched.
